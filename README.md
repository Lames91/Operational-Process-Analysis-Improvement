# Process 01 – Resubmission Analysis

## Overview

This case study analyzes operational activity data collected throughout January to evaluate the frequency, causes, and time impact of activity resubmissions within a business process.

The analysis aims to understand how resubmissions affect overall case completion time, identify the main reasons behind repeated submissions, quantify the resulting delays, and determine opportunities to prevent avoidable rework.

## Analytical Scope

### 1. Resubmission Volume & Frequency

Established an overview of process activity and resubmission patterns by measuring:

- Total number of cases and activities.
- Number of cases containing resubmitted activities.
- Number of activities associated with resubmissions.
- Total resubmission occurrences.
- Activity resubmission rate.
- Total delay days resulting from resubmissions.

### 2. Resubmission Delay Impact

Measured the additional processing time associated with resubmissions by calculating the elapsed time between an activity's return and its subsequent resubmission.

The analysis examined:

- Total accumulated delay days across resubmissions.
- Average delay days by activity.
- Average delay days by resubmission reason.

### 3. Case Completion Time Comparison

Evaluated whether cases involving resubmissions experienced longer completion times compared with cases without resubmissions.

The comparison included:

- Average case completion time.
- Median case completion time.
- Completion time for cases with resubmissions versus cases without resubmissions.

This comparison helped assess the relationship between repeated activity submissions and overall case processing duration.

### 4. Avoidable Resubmission Analysis

Identified resubmissions associated with potentially preventable causes to quantify improvement opportunities.

The analysis covered:

- Total avoidable resubmissions.
- Avoidable resubmission rate relative to total resubmissions.
- Delay days associated with avoidable resubmissions.
- Distribution of avoidable resubmissions by activity.
- Distribution by responsible department.
- Distribution by resubmission reason.

## Business Objectives

- Understand the operational impact of repeated activity submissions.
- Identify recurring resubmission causes.
- Quantify additional processing time associated with rework.
- Highlight the difference in case completion time between cases with and without resubmissions.
- Identify preventable sources of operational delays.
- Support targeted recommendations addressing individual resubmission causes.

## Deliverables

- Interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Resubmission performance analysis.
- Avoidable resubmission analysis.
- Data-driven findings and improvement recommendations.

## Expected Business Value

The analysis provides operational stakeholders with visibility into resubmission patterns, their contribution to processing delays, and opportunities to reduce avoidable rework through targeted corrective actions.

*Note: Process identifiers, activity names, department names, and operational data have been anonymized for portfolio presentation.*
